About This 1 Minute Timer

A single minute is the most-searched timer length in the world, largely because of fitness intervals and classroom activities. It is short enough that people will actually do the thing rather than negotiate with themselves first.

What People Use a 1 Minute Timer For

  • Timed exercise sets: push-ups, burpees, jump rope or mountain climbers
  • One-minute speeches, elevator pitches and interview practice answers
  • Rest periods between heavy lifting sets
  • Board game turns and quick-fire quiz rounds
  • Letting pasta water return to a boil after adding the pasta
  • Meditation and breathing exercises for beginners

How to Use It

The timer above is already running. When it reaches zero, VClock plays an alarm sound, flashes the display and updates the browser tab title so you notice even if you have switched to another window. Press Stop Alarm to silence it, or Reset to run the same 1 minute again.

You can change the alarm sound before or during the countdown — pick from Beep, Digital, Alarm Bell, Chime, Siren or Soft Wake. Selecting one plays a short preview so you can check it is loud enough for your room. If you need a different length, press Reset and enter any duration, or open the main timer page.

Tips for Reliable Timing

  • Click once on the page. Browsers block audio until you interact, so a single click guarantees the alarm will be audible.
  • Bookmark this page. It becomes a one-click 1 minute timer you can reach any time.
  • Keep the tab open. Closing it stops the countdown; switching tabs does not.
  • Check your volume. Both device volume and per-tab mute settings affect whether you hear the alarm.

1 Minute Timer FAQ

Does this 1 minute timer start automatically?

Yes. The countdown begins the moment the page finishes loading, so you do not need to press anything. If you prefer to start it manually, press Reset and then Start.

Will the alarm sound when the 1 minute are up?

Yes. VClock plays your chosen alarm sound, flashes the display and changes the browser tab title. Click anywhere on the page once so the browser allows audio.

Can I pause this timer?

Yes. Press Pause at any point to hold the remaining time exactly where it is, then Resume to continue or Reset to start the 1 minute again from the beginning.

Does it keep counting if I switch to another tab?

Yes. The remaining time is calculated from the real clock rather than a tick counter, so background throttling cannot make it drift.

Can I change the duration?

Yes. Press Reset and type any hours, minutes and seconds you like, or pick a different length from the main timer page.

Is this timer free?

Completely. There is no sign-up, no download, no advertisement wall and no limit on how many times you use it.
