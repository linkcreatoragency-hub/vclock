Choosing the Right Wake-Up Time

The best alarm time is the one that gives you enough sleep and that you can keep to every day. Adults generally need seven to nine hours, so the practical method is to work backwards: decide when you must be up, subtract eight hours, and treat that as your bedtime rather than a suggestion.

Set a Bedtime Alarm Too

Most people who struggle to wake up do not have a morning problem — they have an evening one. Setting an alarm for 9 PM, 10 PM or 11 PM to begin winding down does more for a difficult morning than any change to the morning itself.

Preset Alarms FAQ

How do these preset alarm pages work?

Each page opens with the time already filled in. Press Set Alarm, keep the tab open, and VClock rings at that time with your chosen sound.

Can I set a time that isn't listed?

Yes. Use the main alarm clock and type any hour and minute you like — the presets are just shortcuts for the most common times.

Do I need an account?

No. VClock never asks you to register. Alarms are stored in your own browser and nothing is uploaded.

What is the best alarm time for waking up?

That depends on when you go to bed. Most adults need seven to nine hours, so count back from your alarm: a 6 AM alarm means being asleep by about 10 PM. Consistency matters more than the specific hour.
