Set Alarm for 12:00 PM

A midday alarm is a reminder, not a wake-up — lunch, medication, a call or the end of a long focus block. The alarm below is pre-set for noon.

--:--

Why Set an Alarm for 12:00 PM?

Noon alarms are usually about interrupting yourself rather than waking up. That is exactly when a browser alarm beats a phone: you are already at the computer, and a single tab quietly counting down is easier to trust than remembering to look at a clock.

12:00 PM Across US Time Zones

Booking around 12:00 PM Eastern? Convert it to your own zone first — setting an alarm for the wrong one is the most common way to miss an early call:

Alarm for 12:00 PM — FAQ

Is 12:00 PM noon or midnight?

12:00 PM is noon; 12:00 AM is midnight. The convention confuses almost everyone, which is why timetables often write 12 noon and 12 midnight instead.

Why use an alarm for lunch?

Because deep work removes your sense of time. A noon alarm is the simplest way to stop a morning block from quietly eating the afternoon.

Is the 12:00 PM alarm already set up?

Yes — the hour and minute are ready. All that remains is choosing a sound and pressing Set Alarm, after which VClock waits for 12:00 PM on its own.

Will the 12:00 PM alarm work if I close the tab?

No. The alarm runs inside the page, so the tab must stay open. You can switch freely between other tabs and windows; just do not close this one or quit the browser.

Can I snooze the 12:00 PM alarm?

Yes. When it rings, press Snooze and VClock creates a new alarm nine minutes later — 12:09 PM — exactly like a bedside clock.

Which alarm sound is best for 12:00 PM?

Alarm Bell and Siren are the loudest and best for deep sleep. Chime and Soft Wake are gentler, which suits a shared room or a nap. Each sound previews when you select it.

Need a Countdown Instead?

Use the VClock timer for durations like 10 or 20 minutes rather than a fixed clock time.
